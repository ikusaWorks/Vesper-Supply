James Dawley. Horizontal layout with slogan, address and icons, in three background treatments.

To install one:
  1. Click Open to copy — it opens on its own page.
  2. Select all (Cmd + A), copy (Cmd + C).
  3. Paste into your mail client’s signature editor.
    Gmail: Settings → See all settings → Signature.
    Outlook: Settings → Mail → Compose and reply → Email signature.
    Apple Mail: Settings → Signatures.
