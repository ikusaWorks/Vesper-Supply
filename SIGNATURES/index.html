To install one:
  1. Click Open to copy — the signature opens on its own page.
  2. Select everything on that page (Cmd + A) and copy (Cmd + C).
  3. Paste into your mail client’s signature editor.
    Gmail: Settings → See all settings → Signature.
    Outlook: Settings → Mail → Compose and reply → Email signature.
    Apple Mail: Settings → Signatures, then paste in.
