About us

Where industry meets execution.

Vesper Supply is a U.S.-based industrial procurement and supply company headquartered in Houston, Texas. We serve energy and industrial operators by providing dependable access to critical equipment from trusted manufacturers.

Our capabilities span a broad range of oilfield, electrical, instrumentation, automation, flow control, and other critical industrial equipment. From sourcing and supplier coordination to documentation, export, logistics, and final delivery, Vesper manages the process from source to site with reliability, transparency, and accountability.

With on-the-ground operating capabilities in Venezuela, Vesper combines a strong U.S. sourcing network with broader global procurement capabilities, cross-border logistics, and local execution to support energy and industrial projects across the region.

Who we serve

  • 01

    Upstream production & well operations

    Equipment supporting drilling, production, well intervention, artificial lift, wellhead operations, pressure control, instrumentation and field automation.

  • 02

    Processing, refining & petrochemical

    Flow control, instrumentation, automation, electrical and critical equipment supporting processing facilities, crude upgrading, refining and petrochemical operations.

  • 03

    Pipelines, storage & field infrastructure

    Valves, piping-related equipment, instrumentation, electrical systems, cable and control equipment supporting transportation, storage and field infrastructure.

  • 04

    Power & industrial facilities

    Electrical, instrumentation, automation, control and industrial equipment supporting power generation and other critical industrial operations.

Mission & vision

What we are here to do — and where we are going.

One company, one route, and a standard we hold on both ends of it.

Mission

To provide energy operators with dependable access to critical industrial equipment through trusted sourcing, disciplined execution, and reliable delivery.

With a strong focus on Venezuela and the wider region, Vesper brings together established manufacturers, disciplined procurement, and dependable cross-border delivery to help energy operators move critical projects forward with confidence.

Vision

Bridging leading manufacturers with energy markets across the Americas.

To become a trusted international procurement partner connecting leading manufacturers with critical energy and industrial operations across Venezuela, Latin America, and beyond. Recognized for reliability, transparency, and disciplined execution across every stage of the supply chain.
