About us

A procurement company built for one route.

Vesper Supply is a United States supply company based in Texas. We sell instrumentation, automation, electrical equipment, smart valves, cable and industrial hardware to oil and gas companies, and we exist to connect American manufacturing with operators in Venezuela and the wider region.

Cross-border industrial procurement usually breaks down in the gaps: a distributor who cannot confirm origin, a shipment held for missing paperwork, a warranty that evaporates because the goods moved through an unauthorized channel. Those gaps are the whole job, and they are what we are organized around.

We are an American company buying from American manufacturers. That is a deliberately narrow position, and it is what lets us stand behind the origin, the warranty and the documentation on everything we ship.

Mission & vision

What we are here to do — and where we are going.

One company, one route, and a standard we hold on both ends of it.

Mission

To provide oil and gas companies with reliable, high-quality equipment made in the USA, backed by trustworthy service and fast delivery.

Every quote reflects real factory lead times, and every shipment carries the documentation to prove what it is and where it came from. We would rather give you a date we can hold than the date you want to hear.

Vision

To be the bridge between American manufacturing and South American energy.

We are building a supply route that industrial operators can rely on as infrastructure rather than improvise around. The operators there deserve the same directness a buyer in Houston takes for granted: a real manufacturer behind the part, and a real warranty behind the manufacturer.
